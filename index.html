Questions

Good to know.

Does it come with channels?

No. Doodcom Player is a media player only. You add the playlist (Xtream Codes or M3U) from a provider you already use. Please only add playlists you have the right to watch.

How much does it cost?

Pricing will be on the Google Play page when the app is released. There are no adverts.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional and only used to bring your setup to your other devices. Without one, everything stays on your device.

Which devices does it work on?

Android TV boxes, Fire TV sticks, Formuler boxes, the NVIDIA Shield, and Android phones and tablets.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings, then Sync, then Delete account. You can also follow the steps on the delete account page.

I need help.

Email support@doodcom.com and we'll get back to you.
